# Scope and falsification checks

**1 October 2026.** This is an exploratory derivation audit, not a preregistered observational or numerical fit. The September seed polynomial and its earlier passing controls were known before this work. The proposed algebraic identities were derived before the new arithmetic checks; no novelty claim follows from a passing run.

## Inputs and assumptions

- The public September 6 routed seed, with its positive momentum-squared vertex convention and stripped UV pole, is the input; its scope remains one specified diagram.
- Metric `(+---)`, explicitly declared incoming/outgoing Fourier waves, and a positive diagonal Takagi sterile mass matrix are used.
- Sources are the equations of the declared action. Setting them to zero is a separate free-field limit, rather than reduction of an interacting EFT.
- Flavor products keep their order. Majorana bilinears use transpose; source couplings can also contain Hermitian conjugation as dictated by the action.
- First-order local field-redefinition equivalence is used where stated. The finite quadratic-block control retains the extra quadratic contact that first-order truncation omits.

## Acceptance and failure rules

The local representative must reproduce the independently expressed routed integral polynomial for arbitrary rational off-shell momenta and signed scalar mass parameters. Its gamma matrices must satisfy the Minkowski Clifford algebra, and the gauge ambiguity must pass a Ward test while retaining a nonzero matrix element. Incorrect Fourier signs, the old common-momentum shortcut, and a wrong curvature sign must be distinguishable where tested.

The sterile reduction must hold with arbitrary source fields and explicit EOM residuals, not only homogeneous free solutions. Each omitted source must be detected by a nonzero residual. The static correlated-contact check must work for complex rectangular flavor matrices and fail when the contact or transpose orientation is wrong. A finite triangular change of the sterile variable must preserve the effective Schur block when its quadratic contact is retained and fail without it.

Arithmetic controls check the implemented identities on the selected exact inputs. The analytic notes supply their general derivations under the assumptions. No count of passing cases proves completeness of a Green basis, a full loop calculation, an anomalous-dimension matrix, finite matching, external novelty, or unification.

## Publication boundary

Only newly written derivations and scripts based on public scientific sources enter this checkpoint. The private source inventory is retained separately. Publication is through the repository review workflow; a prepared branch or package does not establish a merged release, live website deployment, or new Zenodo DOI.
