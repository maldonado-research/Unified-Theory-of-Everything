# One evanescent convention for the four-lepton contact

The [ordered triangle residue](../../checkpoint_2026_10_23/seed_yukawa/FOUR_LEPTON_POLE.md) fixes a four-dimensional vector-current pole. Its finite translation into a current coefficient also depends on how the spin tensors are continued away from four dimensions. For this single graph one can exhibit that dependence and identify a momentum difference in which it cancels.

## Ordered open spin chains

Let `d=4−2ε`, with the earlier metric `(+---)`. Suppress the flavor labels `αβγδ` on the following two operators. The sums are over weak indices `i,j`. In this note `J_d` denotes an entire current *product*.

```text
P_d = Σij (bar Lα^i C_D (bar Lβ^j)^T) ((Lγ^i)^T C_D^{-1} Lδ^j)
J_d = Σij (bar Lα^i γμ Lγ^i) (bar Lβ^j γ^μ Lδ^j).
```

The transpose acts on spin components and the displayed odd fields retain their order. Choose the indicated ordered open chains as lifts. Use the Clifford anticommutator with the d-dimensional metric, an anticommuting γ5 on the open lines, and the transpose rules `C_D (γμ)^T C_D^{-1}=−γμ`, `C_D γ5^T C_D^{-1}=γ5`, `C_D^T=−C_D`. Left fields and barred left fields carry the usual left and right projectors. Leave the two chains in their displayed pairings until the physical projection. This prescription suffices for the selected diagram with a scalar Majorana numerator. It calls for neither a closed chiral trace nor a Fierz relation in non-integer dimension. Other diagrams can require more evanescents and prescriptions.

To take the physical projection, use the chiral matrices and `C_D=iγ²γ⁰=diag(e,−e)` with `e12=1` and `e21=−1`. The barred and unbarred contractions in `P_d` each restrict to `−e`, giving the positive ordered Weyl tensor `P₄`. Pauli completeness gives `2 e_rt e_uv` for the two-current spin contraction. Moving the middle odd fields to the order `[bar α, bar β, γ, δ]` multiplies it by minus one. Consequently `J₄=−2P₄` for each fixed weak-index pair. For spin slots `(r,t,u,v)=(1,2,1,2)` in one-based indexing, the coefficients are respectively `P₄=1` and `J₄=−2`. The [checker](verify_fierz_scheme.py) evaluates all 16 components.

## What a change of convention does

Keep the flavor tensor complex. Define `R=Y* M Y†` and `h=Cαβ Rγδ/[2(16π²)]`. The common scale factors in dimensional regularization are the same in both conventions and suppressed. Every action below includes a sum over these flavor indices and its Hermitian conjugate. Choose a real dimensionless parameter `a` and define

```text
E_a = P_d + (1/2 + a ε) J_d.
```

Every member has zero physical tree projection at ε=0. For the chosen ordered continuation the local cancelling action is

```text
− h P_d/ε = h (J_d/2 − E_a)/ε + h a J_d.
```

Thus the physical current pole is `+Cαβ Rγδ/[4(16π²)ε]` in the counterterm. The finite term `+h a J_d` converts the simple-pole subtraction in these coordinates back to the same ordered counterterm. The distinction between changing coordinates and changing what is subtracted fixes both signs:

| Quantity compared at fixed ordered tensors | Change from a to b |
| --- | --- |
| Evanescent tensor | `E_b − E_a = (b−a) ε J_d` |
| Simple-pole counterterm alone | `−h(b−a) J_d` |
| Added finite counterterm for the original ordered prescription | `+h(b−a) J_d` |
| Sum of the preceding two counterterm changes | `0` |

As an example, increasing the parameter from zero to one adds `+Cαβ Rγδ/[2(16π²)]` to the finite current coefficient required to preserve the common subtraction. Its conjugate appears in the conjugate flavor channel. Doubling the displayed complex coefficient would give the wrong Hermitian continuation. The translation is a convention-dependent local term. An absolute finite Wilson coefficient would also require a specified matching calculation and other contributions to the same operator.

There is additional freedom in the lifts themselves. For regular local tensors `X,Y`, set `P'_d=P_d+εX` and `J'_d=J_d+εY`. Then

```text
E'_b − E_a = ε [X + Y/2 + (b−a) J_d] + ε² bY.
```

Holding the numerical pole residue fixed while replacing the ordered lift by the primed one changes `−h P_d/ε` by `−hX`. A common bare action requires compensating that change. The original physical-spinor pole alone cannot distinguish those lifts or supply the finite parts of other operators. This calculation addresses the direction generated by the specified scalar and vector open chains. Evanescent insertions into later loops require further renormalization data.

## Which part survives subtraction at two momenta

Take the projected scalar coefficient after stripping the same ordered spin and weak tensor at each kinematic point. A local dimension-six current counterterm adds a constant to this reduced coefficient. Hence

```text
[K(Q²) + c] − [K(0) + c] = K(Q²) − K(0).
```

For the [triangle benchmark](FINITE_TRIANGLE.md), the ordered integral difference is UV convergent before the four-dimensional spin projection. At positive scalar mass the indicated Euclidean kinematics also permits a zero sterile mass in the scalar kernel. An order-ε lift multiplying a finite difference vanishes in the limit. A higher-derivative local input proportional to `Q²` would survive this subtraction. Projecting actual spinor wavefunctions at two different momenta without stripping their tensor also changes what is compared. The benchmark gives the momentum dependence of the specified off-shell graph in its chosen field coordinates.
