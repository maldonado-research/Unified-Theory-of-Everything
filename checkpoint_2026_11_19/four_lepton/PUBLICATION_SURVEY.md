The TOE archival reference is N00AK-r1. It establishes the exact tree-level Majorana kernel and explains why the two derivative Wick pairings require their own momentum routing. Extracting a loop coefficient requires an actual integral with those routings. [TOE archival record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22319172)

HDblast concerns a five-dimensional cosmological shell. Its 19 November edition adds a second time-dependent scalar Yukawa source acting on the same quantum state. The two production amplitudes interfere. Its README also describes a control that changes the relative phase of two frequency bands while holding every frequency's energy fixed. A phase-sensitive readout can therefore distinguish inputs whose energy spectra agree. The served project page gives the cosmological question, and the pinned repository and archival edition give the dated result. [HDblast website](https://maldonado-research.github.io/HDblast/) · [Pinned README](https://github.com/maldonado-research/HDblast/blob/c2e8ccb69ae4e48ca7860a59e31a8bcf2969bfad/README.md) · [19 November archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24376497)

The analogy here concerns the order of operations. For the selected four-lepton kernel, each internal Majorana mass eigenstate contributes a complex flavor tensor weighted by its own mass and loop function. The contributions add coherently at the amplitude level. Taking a modulus or squaring each term before summing discards relative phases. This can be tested within the EFT by comparing the matrix-resolvent calculation to its sum in a mass eigenbasis using complex flavor rotations and unequal masses. The shell calculation describes massless Dirac production on prescribed histories. Its rates, energy bounds and coupling normalization supply no matching relation for the Majorana kernel.
