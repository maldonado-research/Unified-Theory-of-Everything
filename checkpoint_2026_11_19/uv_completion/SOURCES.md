## The scalar box and the two-boson threshold

Pham studied the reverse process, two electrons scattering into two muons, with Majorana neutrinos and electroweak bosons. The low-energy loop functions separate the diagrams with two vector bosons, two Goldstone bosons and one of each. Cannoni, Kolb and Panella subsequently evaluated the box functions with full external momenta. Their Fig. 1(b) and Eq. (4) of the versioned PDF give the scalar contribution. After factoring the four scalar couplings, its mass dependence is proportional to $M_i M_j$, the external spinor product, and $D_0(s,t)+D_0(s,u)$, with the usual loop factor $1/(4\pi)^2$. They discuss the imaginary part above the two-boson threshold. These articles establish prior calculations of the process and threshold. The scalar in our unbroken gaugeless action is a propagating field. In the electroweak calculation the Goldstone diagram belongs to a gauge-dependent decomposition and the full prediction includes the vector diagrams. [Pham, Eqs. (2)–(6)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0003077v2) · [Cannoni, Kolb & Panella, Fig. 1 and Eqs. (1)–(7)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0209120v2)

Cvetič and colleagues give two distinct flavor contractions for the crossed muonium–antimuonium system. Their Eq. (10) contains a product requiring each of two neutrino labels to couple to both flavors, and a Majorana term with a squared electron coupling at one label and a squared muon coupling at the other. Disjoint flavor support removes the first structure and permits the second. Asaka and Tsuyuki's broken-phase electron–electron to W-boson pair amplitude gives a separate tree check. Its Appendix A, Eqs. (A3)–(A5), explicitly retains $M_I/(t-M_I^2)$ and the crossed transfer. Their vertices include the charged-current mixing and polarization factors. [Cvetič et al., Eq. (10) and Figs. 1–2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0504126v2) · [Asaka & Tsuyuki, Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1508.04937v4)

## Inverse mass moments and stability

Elgaard-Clausen and Trott obtain a dimension-seven coefficient proportional to the Yukawa bilinear divided by the cube of the heavy mass, with a one-half in their action normalization. Section 3.3 also gives operators induced after the field equations are used. Lu gives the full Euclidean anomalous response $A(Q^2)=\sum_i M_i y_i y_i^T/(Q^2+M_i^2)$ and the inverse odd mass moments through every derivative order. The paragraph following Eq. (54) explicitly describes how distinct mass supports can cancel in the lowest moment while persisting in the full sequence. Hence the zero at zero transfer and a finite slope are established features of tree-level Majorana exchange. The calculation here specializes them to an assigned mass and flavor texture and checks the size of the finite-transfer correction in a physical scalar cut. [Elgaard-Clausen & Trott, Eq. (3.15) in the v3 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.04415v3) · [Lu, Eqs. (17), (24)–(26), (54)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.00888v1)

Kersten and Smirnov impose a two-singlet cancellation at one threshold in the Standard Model extension and exhibit its running between unequal singlet thresholds. Their Eq. (34) includes Higgs and electroweak gauge couplings. Moffat, Pascoli and Weiland analyze the all-orders massless-light-neutrino condition under assumptions that include the absence of cancellations between different perturbative orders. The unequal mass pair used here implements a tree matching condition. Applying these papers' numerical loop estimates would require the corresponding electroweak interactions and Higgs phase. A stability claim for the present gaugeless model would require its own loop matching. [Kersten & Smirnov, §2.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.3221v2) · [Moffat, Pascoli & Weiland, §§2.1–2.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.07611v1)

## Recent results and the author's archives

An October 2026 study of a positive-muon collider considers the charge-conjugate external flavors with doubly charged singlet and triplet scalars. Its introduction distinguishes violation of individual flavor numbers from violation of total lepton number. Here the external transition also has $\Delta L_e=-\Delta L_\mu=2$ and $\Delta L=0$, while the two sewn subamplitudes carry opposite lepton-number changes. A November CMS search tests heavy neutral leptons of mass 20–100 GeV in top decays in proton–proton collisions, with same-sign, same-flavor dileptons. Its limits depend on production, decay and weak mixing. Using these studies for a constraint would require a map from the benchmark parameters to their production and decay amplitudes. [Doubly charged scalar model, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.31312v1) · [CMS top-decay search, abstract and §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04904v1)
