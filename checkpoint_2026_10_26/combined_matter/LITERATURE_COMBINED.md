## Off-shell quantum equivalence and probes

Iberê Kuntz and Stefano Liberati, [Off-shell equivalence in quantum field theory and gravity, arXiv:2607.12644v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12644v1), 14 July 2026. The provider returned selected passages from the introduction and conclusions. The authors distinguish the usual on-shell equivalence theorem for admissible local perturbatively invertible field changes from stronger questions about off-shell quantum quantities. Their proposed comparison involves a Vilkovisky–DeWitt effective action and theory data paired with a declared class of probes; branch and auxiliary-field choices also matter.

**Use here:** an explicit first-order action-coordinate identity with transformed interaction and probe sources is a valuable check, but it does not independently establish equivalence of complete quantum effective actions or all off-shell responses. This paper does not calculate the retained-sterile derivative-seed coefficients, provide a tested Vilkovisky–DeWitt construction for this packet, or establish gravity/unification in the present hypothesis. No such construction or observable comparison is performed here.

## Local versus global field maps

Bin Zhu, [Non-injective field redefinitions and quantum inequivalence in scalar theories, arXiv:2607.18166v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.18166v1), 20 July 2026. Selected provider passages describe polynomial scalar examples with a unit local Jacobian but multiple inverse branches. The author separates perturbation theory on a fixed local sheet from global equivalence of the full field space.

**Use here:** specify the perturbative order and local invertibility when interpreting the combined field change. The paper's globally non-injective scalar examples do not show that this specific infinitesimal retained-sterile EFT map is non-injective or quantum-inequivalent. A formal first-order inverse does not by itself prove a global quantum equivalence. No global functional integral or unitary equivalence theorem is claimed for the present map.
