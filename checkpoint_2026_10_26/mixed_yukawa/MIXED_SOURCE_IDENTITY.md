# Source-preserving mixed lepton/Higgs action identity

26 October 2026 (Pacific). This note is a coefficient-independent action identity. It assumes a mixed local vertex proportional to the indicated momentum tensor, but does not calculate or fix the overall loop coefficient. The actual loop integration and its sign must be established separately.

## Conventions and the momentum representative

Take the unbroken theory with `phi=tilde H`, `rho=phi^dagger phi`, covariant derivatives `D_mu`, and an arbitrary complex active-flavor matrix `K`. Define

```text
J_ab^mu = bar L_a gamma^mu L_b,
R_ab = (D_mu bar L_a) gamma^mu L_b,
T_ab = bar L_a gamma^mu D_mu L_b,
A_mu = (D_mu phi)^dagger phi,
W_mu = (D_mu phi)^dagger phi - phi^dagger D_mu phi.
```

Weak indices are contracted within each displayed scalar or lepton bilinear. `rho` and `J` are gauge singlets, so `partial_mu rho=A_mu+phi^dagger D_mu phi` and `partial_mu J_ab^mu=R_ab+T_ab`.

For all fields Fourier-expanded with incoming wave `exp(-ip.x)`, the ordered local representative

```text
O(K) = i K_ab [A_mu J_ab^mu - rho R_ab]                (1)
```

has the desired vector factor `(p_phi-dagger-p_barL)_mu` when the differentiated scalar and lepton are assigned those external momenta. The overall amplitude phase, flavor routing and coefficient must be matched to the independently computed loop before identifying (1) with a pole. In particular, its Hermitian conjugate has the opposite derivative placement and the conjugated flavor coefficient.

## Exact off-shell identity

Write the literal interacting lepton equations and their residuals as

```text
e_b = i gamma^mu D_mu L_b - S_b,
ebar_a = i (D_mu bar L_a) gamma^mu + bar S_a.
```

The sources `S,bar S` are retained independent objects. For the standard reference interaction they include the neutrino source `Y_nu phi N_R` and charged-lepton Yukawa source `Y_e H e_R`; any independently coupled lepton probe belongs in the same source. Their adjoints have their actual conjugate order. No sterile field is eliminated.

Product rules imply

```text
O(K) = i K_ab W_mu J_ab^mu/2
     + rho K_ab [3 bar S_a L_b - bar L_a S_b]/2
     - rho K_ab [3 ebar_a L_b + bar L_a e_b]/2
     + (i/2) partial_mu(rho K_ab J_ab^mu).              (2)
```

This is an **off-shell identity**, not a free-field equation or an assumption that the sources vanish. It is valid for constant `K`; a spacetime-dependent coefficient adds the derivative of `K` to the boundary expansion. All scalar and lepton derivatives are covariant inside `A,W,R,T`, so gauge connection contributions are retained in the identity. Integration drops the final boundary term under the declared boundary conditions.

To see (2), first use `A=(partial rho+W)/2` and the covariant product rule for `J`, then integrate `partial rho J` by parts. This gives the derivative combination `-i rho(3R+T)/2`. Substituting the two residual definitions yields the explicit source and residual terms above. The identity holds componentwise in flavor and weak indices, with no assumption that `K` is Hermitian or normal.

## Conjugate-safe first-order field change

For the reference lepton action, with `S,bar S` held as the declared interaction and external sources, a first-order change obeys

```text
delta S = integral [delta bar L_a e_a - ebar_a delta L_a].
```

The residual in (2), together with its Hermitian conjugate, is generated by

```text
delta L = +(rho/2)(3K-K^dagger)L,
delta bar L = +(rho/2)bar L(3K^dagger-K).              (3)
```

The two changes in (3) are conjugates. Thus an action containing `O(K)+h.c.` can cancel its displayed residual by the **negative** of both shifts in (3). Any common perturbative coefficient multiplying the mixed packet must multiply these changes as well. The retained source terms in (2), their Hermitian conjugates, and the current term then remain. Variations of external probes and every other interaction in the full reference action must be carried consistently; the calculation does not assert a complete quantum effective-action or physical Wilson-basis map.
