26 October 2026 Pacific; retrieval 27 October UTC. The following bounded search addresses operator reduction and the interpretation of a mixed lepton–Higgs loop with the Majorana sterile field retained. It is not an exhaustive review or a novelty determination.

## Lower-order insertions and Green-basis reduction

Álvaro Díaz-Carmona, [Renormalization of the Standard Model effective field theory to dimension eight, arXiv:2602.05461v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05461v1), February 2026. The provider returned passages from chapters 5.5 and 5.6. They state that redundant dimension-six operators can modify physical dimension-seven coefficients when combined with a Weinberg insertion. The same passages describe an off-shell Green basis followed by on-shell reduction, with wave-function renormalization included. They also acknowledge incomplete two-fermion and four-fermion results at dimension eight. These points are useful checks against dropping lower-order insertions or identifying an off-shell pole with a complete physical coefficient.

The paper concerns pure SMEFT; the present calculation keeps a sterile Majorana field and a specified dimension-seven derivative seed. Its anomalous-dimension entries and conclusions about pure-SMEFT field content cannot be copied into this retained-sterile calculation. A direct comparison would first require matched field content, operator normalization, mass and flavor conventions, and the same reduction scheme. The provider's paper passages did not identify their indexed body version; the arXiv abstract page separately confirms that v1 is the available version.

## Matching and observables

Lukáš Gráf, Chandan Hati, Ana Martín-Galán and Oliver Scholer, [Importance of Loop Effects in Probing Lepton Number Violation, arXiv:2504.00081v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00081v2), revised July 2025. In the passages inspected, the authors discuss loop mixing and matching between dimension-five and dimension-seven lepton-number-violating operators and study consequences for neutrinoless double beta decay in stated SMEFT and ultraviolet-model scenarios. Their examples show why both matching and running can matter when connecting coefficients to an observable.

Those model-dependent estimates do not determine the present mixed Majorana triangle, a finite amplitude or a bound on this hypothesis. The provider returned passages from an unversioned indexed body; the arXiv abstract page separately identifies v2 as the current listed version. The link above identifies that version and is not a claim that each excerpt was checked against its full text.

## Retained-sterile and quantum-source cautions

The preceding checkpoint's directly inspected [Zhang, arXiv:2504.00792v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.00792v2), remains the closer comparison for sterile-neutrino field redefinitions and the differentiation of redundant coefficients before projecting them away. Its explicitly studied operator truncation differs from the present dimension-seven seed. The prior [source review](../../checkpoint_2026_10_23/seed_yukawa/LITERATURE_SEED_YUKAWA.md) also discusses the distinction between classical field changes and transformations of quantum effective actions. These earlier body readings were not repeated during this limited search.

None of these references supplies the literal coefficient, the complete physical running vector or an experimental result for the present model. Preserve Majorana mass order, weak and flavor contractions, external-source transport, the chosen subtraction/evanescent conventions, and actual threshold matching before comparing with physical observables.
