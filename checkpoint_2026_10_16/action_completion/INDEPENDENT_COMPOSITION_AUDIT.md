# Independent field-map composition and scalar-Hessian audit

16 October 2026. Input: candidate.8, `5f9889b`, and its declared selected
packet. The derivation and direct Grassmann calculation below were completed
before reading the producer's new composition result. They use the existing
electroweak source map, interacting normalization map, and original six
placements of `O_J`. No new loop integration is performed.

The source-to-normalizing bridge exists as a local first-order map. On an
off-shell constant scalar background, its quadratic action requires a scalar
tadpole contraction. That contraction has the opposite sign to the one
generated by the source-reducing map. An explicit calculation using the
actual packet and actual maps confirms both statements.

## Explicit bridge, with its direction fixed

Write the two old-field substitutions as

    Xi_old=Xi_S+kappa v_S(Xi_S),
    Xi_old=Xi_C+kappa v_C(Xi_C).

All fields and conjugates are included in `Xi`. Their first-order bridge is

    Xi_S=Xi_C+kappa b(Xi_C),       b=v_C-v_S.                 (A1)

Use `P0=-partial^2`, `P=P0+m_H^2`, `A=i slash partial`, and the full
`D=partial+i Acal`. With exactly the coefficients `ell,t,x,y,j,j^I`
of the input source map, the nonzero bridge components are

    b_L=ell-phi F P N_L/2,
    b_phi*=t,                       b_phi=t^dagger,
    b_B,mu=-g'y_L(j_mu+j_mu^dagger)/6,
    b_W,mu^I=-g(j_mu^I+j_mu^{I dag})/6,
    b_NL=x+y^c-P(F^dagger phi^dagger L)/2,
    b_NR=(b_NL)^c.                                        (A2)

The conjugate lepton component is fixed by Hermitian conjugation. Every
quantity on the right is evaluated on the C fields. In particular `P` acts
on the **whole** neutral composite in the sterile shift. The gauge shifts
are real, the W shift is adjoint covariant, and the sterile completion
preserves the Majorana condition with no extra half factor.

Substituting the explicit lepton coefficient simplifies its bridge to

    (b_L)_alpha^a=sum_i F_alpha i [
        i gamma^mu(D_mu phi)^a A N_Li/3
        -phi^a P0 N_Li/3].                                (A3)

Its `m_H^2` terms cancel. This is a useful sign and direction check.
On `phi=(u,0)` at zero gauge background,

    b_nu=-u F P0 N_L/3.                                  (A4)

The sterile bridge retains the source terms in `x+y^c`. Deleting them
would not implement (A1) in the interacting theory.

Let `S_S=S_0+kappa P_red` and `S_C=S_0+kappa P_C`, including Hermitian
conjugates in these abbreviations. The input source identity and direct
normalizing substitution say respectively

    P_red=P_orig+delta_(v_S) S_0,
    P_C=P_orig+delta_(v_C) S_0.

Applying (A1) to the **whole** source-reduced action gives

    S_S[Xi_C+kappa b]=S_0[Xi_C]+kappa(P_red+delta_b S_0)
                    =S_0[Xi_C]+kappa P_C.                (A5)

Thus the induced coefficient lists are not added to one another. They are
alternative coordinate descriptions of the same selected action, related
by the explicit bridge. Variations of other retained EFT operators carry
their own `kappa F C_d` order; those terms are not automatically quadratic
in `kappa`. Sources, gauge fixing, and ghosts must also be transported when
they belong to the object being compared.

## The actual off-shell scalar contribution

For the neutral quadratic calculation take real constant `u`, zero gauge
backgrounds, and zero fermion background. Keep

    V=m_H^2 rho+lambda rho^2,   rho=phi^dagger phi,
    a=m_H^2+2lambda u^2,
    e_phi^(0)=delta S_0/delta phi*=-a u

in the neutral scalar component. No stationarity condition has been used.
The scalar source-map coefficient is the actual bilinear

    t=(1/6)sum_alpha i F_alpha i bar nu_Lalpha A N_Li.     (A6)

The original scalar is `phi_old=u-kappa t^dagger` in source coordinates,
with its conjugate `phi*_old=u-kappa t`. Direct expansion of the potential
through fermion degree two gives

    delta_S L_potential^(2)=+kappa a u(t+t^dagger).        (A7)

The bridge instead has `phi_S=u+kappa t^dagger`, and therefore

    delta_bridge L_potential^(2)=-kappa a u(t+t^dagger).   (A8)

The normalizing map has no scalar shift. Equations (A7)-(A8) are consequently
necessary for (A5) away from `a=0`. The scalar kinetic term has no linear
variation on the constant background. The background gauge equations and
fermion equations vanish, so no other background gradient contributes to
the neutral fermion Hessian at this field degree.

This is the nonlinear-coordinate Hessian term in a concrete form. If one
writes the quadratic fermion action as `psi^T H psi/2` using independent
Grassmann variables and the corresponding antisymmetric coefficient matrix,
the full pullback has both the fermion-Jacobian congruence and the scalar
background gradient contracted with the second derivative of the scalar
map. Equivalently, without derivative-convention ambiguities, its extra
quadratic polynomial is exactly (A7) or (A8). A linear fermion Jacobian
alone misses that polynomial. At the stationary background `a=0`, it
vanishes, as the earlier caveat anticipated.

For the concrete off-shell witness below, `u=3/2`, `m_H^2=7/3`,
`lambda=2/5`, hence `a=62/15` and

    delta_S L_potential^(2)/kappa=(31/30)
                 [sum F_alpha i bar nu_Lalpha A N_Li+h.c.]. (A9)

The bridge contributes the negative of (A9). It is a nonzero polynomial,
not a boundary term. This also supplies a normalization check on the
`1/6` in the actual scalar shift.

## Actual quadratic source representative and field substitutions

The independent calculation retains the full real symmetric Majorana mass
and the renormalizable sources. On this background define

    S_L=uY N_R,          S_L^N=uY^dagger nu_L,
    S_R=(S_L^N)^c,
    R=M N_R+S_R,
    T=M^2 N_L+M S_L^N+A S_R,
    U=M^3 N_R+M^2 S_R+M A S_L^N+A^2 S_R,
    c=2m_H^2/3+lambda u^2/3.

The complete quadratic part of the displayed source-reduced packet is

    P_red^(2)=5u/6 [bar nu_L U]_F+c u[bar nu_L R]_F
       +u/3[(K bar S_L)R]_F-u/6[bar S_L T]_F
       +m_H^2 u/2[bar S_L N_L]_F+h.c.                     (A10)

The scalar-source insertion has four fermions and does not enter (A10).
The curvature terms vanish on the chosen zero-gauge background. Current
products have at least four fermions; the scalar current is zero here.
These facts justify the quadratic truncation, without deleting a source
from the underlying interacting identity.

Directly evaluating the original six placements gives

    P_orig^(2)=u[bar nu_L A(P0+m_H^2)N_L]_F+h.c.          (A11)

The field map used in the check is not an arbitrary linear matrix. It is
the actual source-map linearization:

    ell=u F(P0/6+m_H^2/2)N_L,
    x=u[(5/6)M^2 F^dagger+F^dagger(5P0/6+c)]nu_L
          +u^2 F^dagger Y A N_R/6,
    y=5u M F^dagger A nu_L/6-u^2 M F^dagger Y N_R/6,
    v_S,nu=-ell,             v_S,NL=-(x+y^c).            (A12)

The normalizing linearization is

    v_C,nu=-u F(P0+m_H^2)N_L/2,
    v_C,NL=-u F^dagger(P0+m_H^2)nu_L/2.                 (A13)

All orders in (A12) matter; `M` is not commuted through `F^dagger` or `Y`.
The code constructs the Majorana four-spinor `N=N_L+N_L^c`, varies both
slots of its half-normalized kinetic and mass action, and conjugates
Grassmann products by reversing their order. It then verifies directly

    P_red^(2)=P_orig^(2)+delta_(v_S,fermion)S_0^(2)
                            +a u(t+t^dagger),
    P_red^(2)+delta_(b,fermion)S_0^(2)-a u(t+t^dagger)
                       =P_orig^(2)+delta_(v_C,fermion)S_0^(2). (A14)

The normalizing kinetic variation cancels (A11), while a nonzero correlated
mass/Yukawa remainder survives. Thus the equality is stronger than merely
repeating a generic chain rule or checking the kinetic block alone.
