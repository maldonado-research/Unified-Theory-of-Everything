# Restricted hypercharge matching sector

Research candidate, 15 October 2026. This note defines the scope of a one-gauge-leg extension of the public routed seed calculation. No completed operator basis, physical running coefficient or observational prediction is claimed.

Use the unbroken electroweak phase, a massless lepton doublet L with hypercharge y_L=-1/2, a scalar doublet H with y_H=+1/2 and signed squared-mass parameter m_H², and gauge-singlet sterile fermions. The reference interactions are the sterile Yukawa and the singlet derivative seed. The doublet contraction is J_alpha=epsilon_ij L_alpha^i H^j. The seed convention remains the public positive-momentum-squared C_(7,seed) vertex. For exp(-ipx), a literal +(1/2) J^T W1 Box J corresponds to C_(7,seed)=-W1 in the matching convention; this translation is necessary before using any pole in an action.

Project onto exactly one insertion of C_(7,seed), exactly one conjugate sterile Yukawa, one hypercharge gauge coupling, and no additional scalar quartic, charged-lepton/quark Yukawa, nonabelian coupling or independent physical dimension-six insertion. Those exclusions define a coefficient of a formal multivariate coupling expansion. They do not assert that the excluded terms vanish in the complete effective theory. Masses are fixed parameters rather than additional inverse-heavy-scale suppression. There is no internal sterile line in the two diagrams; a sterile mass therefore cannot appear directly in their loop denominators.

For a connected graph with L loops and vertex valences d_v, E=2-2L+sum_v(d_v-2). One four-field seed, one cubic Yukawa and one cubic hypercharge vertex at one loop give E=4. The selected 1PI external fields are sterile, lepton, scalar and gauge. The hypercharge vertex attaches either to the internal lepton or the internal scalar of the known bubble, giving two triangle integrals. External-leg emissions are reducible and are not part of this 1PI function. The whole composite J has zero hypercharge, so its ordinary Box has no independent gauge connection; introducing an extra seed gauge vertex without changing the action would double count the cancellation between charged constituents.

Five- and six-leg functions at one loop require additional explicitly declared vertices or different operator insertions. They remain necessary in larger coupling sectors and in a complete sourced EOM reduction; their absence from this fixed coefficient is not completeness of the dimension-seven theory.

Use dimensional regularization d=4-2epsilon and generic off-shell external momenta. Separate UV poles from IR poles before taking any massless or on-shell limit. Extract only the coefficient of the simple UV pole. The open fermion chain has no anomalous closed gamma5 trace; four-dimensional matrix representatives may check the residue after d-dependent pole coefficients are derived. They cannot determine evanescent finite terms, a complete subtraction scheme, or higher-loop effects. Counterterm signs must be translated separately from stripped integral signs.

The required progression is: compute both triangle numerators; sum their poles; compare the contracted result with the difference of the known three-point polynomial; subtract the one-gauge kernel of the previously declared local representative; test the remaining transverse tensor independently. A Ward identity fixes the longitudinal response but does not determine the transverse remainder. Matching that remainder to a complete independent operator basis, sourcing its EOM descendants and combining all other counterterms remain further work.
