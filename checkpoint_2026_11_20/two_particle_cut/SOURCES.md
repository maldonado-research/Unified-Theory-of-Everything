## A signed vertex check at spacelike momentum

Write $B=M_k^2$ for the squared mass on the internal Majorana line and let $s'$ denote the virtuality entering the three-point function. For massless doublet fields, Buchmüller and Plümacher give a matrix vertex with two momentum structures. The term proportional to the on-shell lepton momentum vanishes against the external spinor. The remaining coefficient $b_k$ has the following absorptive part at $s'>0$. Their Eq. (57) contains the relative minus sign in $\lambda(1-M b K^T M)$ at a sterile pole, where $K=\lambda^\dagger\lambda$. [Buchmüller and Plümacher, Eqs. (13)–(18) and (57)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9710460v2)

$$
\operatorname{Im} b_k(s'+i0)=\frac{1-(1+B/s')\ln(1+s'/B)}{16\pi s'}.
$$

An independent Euclidean check follows by dispersion. The finite triangle decays at large virtuality. Insert its cut into $b_k(-Q)=\pi^{-1}\int_0^\infty ds'\,\mathrm{Im}\,b_k(s'+i0)/(s'+Q)$ for $Q>0$. In the numerator use $(1+1/x)\ln(1+x)-1=x\int_0^1(1-u)\,du/(1+xu)$, with $x=s'/B$. The $s'$ integral gives

$$
16\pi^2 b_k(-Q)=-\int_0^1 du\,(1-u)\frac{\ln[B/(Qu)]}{B-Qu},
\qquad 16\pi^2B b_k(-B)=-1.
$$

The coincident point $Qu=B$ has a removable singularity. At $Q=B$ the integrand reduces to $\ln(1/u)/B$, including its integrable endpoint. In the benchmark convention $V=-16\pi^2 b_k$, this yields $V(B,B;0)=1/B$. Keeping a nonzero common scalar mass separately gives $aV(a,a;a)=\pi^2/8-\ln 2$ at the lightest threshold. The latter identity follows from the massive Feynman integral. The angular unitarity calculation extends the comparison through the spacelike interval for the four benchmark masses. ## Propagators, external states and exchange graphs

* Buchmüller and Plümacher write the inverse sterile propagator as $\not q-M-\Sigma(q)$. The two chiral self-energies contain transposed Yukawa matrices. For massless internal doublets their coefficient in modified minimal subtraction is $[\ln(|q^2|/\mu^2)-2-i\pi\,\Theta(q^2)]/(16\pi^2)$. This constrains the sign, the constant and the logarithm of the sterile insertion. Pilaftsis gives a separate check of the factor $(1-M_H^2/p^2)^2$ for an open massive-scalar channel. His charged Goldstone and neutral Higgs have different masses, so his channel multiplicities require conversion for a degenerate unbroken doublet. [Buchmüller and Plümacher, Eqs. (3)–(12)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9710460v2) · [Pilaftsis, Eqs. (4.2)–(4.3) and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9707235v1)
* Roulet, Covi and Vissani show how the crossed tree exchange enters the interference terms and explain by hypercharge why independent one-loop boxes preserve lepton number in the crossed $\ell H$ problem. Their flavor-summed CP asymmetry obeys a cancellation. The signed off-diagonal transition amplitude considered here has a different projection. Atwood, Bar-Shalom and Soni calculate the tree-level like-sign charged-Higgs pair cross section mediated by a Majorana particle and give its $\beta=\sqrt{1-4m_H^2/s}$ dependence. [Roulet et al., p. 4, footnote 2 and Fig. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9712468v1) · [Atwood et al., Eq. (8)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0701005v2)
* Ohlsson and Pernow use both scalar assignments in the tree Majorana exchange and derive external lepton and scalar wave-function matching factors from two-point functions. Their expanded expressions use degenerate heavy masses and low external scales. The residue check here retains each mass separately, including the coincident scalar and lightest-singlet threshold.
