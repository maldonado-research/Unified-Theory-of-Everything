# Research comments and contributions

Specific objections, failed reproductions and alternative derivations are welcome. Identify the exact file, equation or release and explain which assumption or step you are examining. For numerical problems, include software versions, the minimal input and a concise error report without credentials or private account information.

For proposed physical extensions, state the action, conventions, interaction content and observable being calculated. A new pattern or successful fit should be labeled exploratory unless it has faced an appropriately prospective test.

Keep the published baseline intact. Proposed changes should preserve provenance and distinguish source corrections from new results. Contributions to the author-owned archive use its CC BY 4.0 terms unless explicitly agreed otherwise; external dependencies and quoted research retain their own licenses. A contribution or merge is not a claim of independent peer review.
